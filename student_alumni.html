For students & alumni

Helpful links for the Neuro-X students and alumni alike.

Student Guide

Navigating the EPFL campus is easy with the interactive campus map. Be sure to also keep track of important deadlines, exam sessions, and holidays by checking the official academic calendar.

When selecting your classes, you can find valuable student feedback on various courses through Course Finder. For academic and personal support throughout your studies, EPFL provides a comprehensive collection of resources for students.

Finally, finding accommodation is a crucial step for a smooth academic journey. Explore the EPFL housing portal to find accommodation options, advice, and guidance for living around the campus area.

Alumni Network

Join a global community of graduates by exploring the main EPFL Alumni webpage. Staying connected allows you to build a powerful network, participate in reunions, and give back through mentorship opportunities for current students.

As a graduate, you gain access to a wide range of exclusive alumni advantages that support your lifelong connection to the school and your fellow peers across various industries.

To keep up with networking events, connect with colleagues on the official EPFL Alumni LinkedIn page. You can also discover inspiring success stories and see the diverse paths graduates have taken by following their Instagram account.

Career Resources

Take the next step in your professional journey by exploring the Alumni Job Board or browsing the latest EPFL job openings. For broader networking, don't miss the Forum EPFL, recognized as one of the largest student-run recruitment fairs in Europe.

To ensure your applications stand out, you can consult these official guides on how to craft an impactful Resume and a compelling Cover Letter.

Further enhance your skills by participating in specialized career trainings and attending networking events hosted by EPFLcareer, designed to prepare you for the competitive job market.
