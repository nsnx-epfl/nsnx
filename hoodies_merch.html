Show your Neuro-X pride with our exclusive association merchandise. Our collections are designed to celebrate our interdisciplinary community while providing high-quality apparel for students and faculty alike.

How to Order

1. Pre-Order (October)

We open a specific Google Form once a year in October. Make sure to follow our Instagram or check the newsletter to catch the announcement before the deadline!

2. Payment & Validation

Finalize your order at our dedicated stand in the SV Building. We accept both Twint and Cash payments during the validation week.

3. Collection (December)

Once production is complete in early December, you will receive an email invitation to collect your hoodie directly from the NSNX team.
