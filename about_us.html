What is NSNX?

The NeuroStudents’ NetworX (NSNX) is the official student association for the Neuro-X Master’s program at EPFL. It fosters a sense of belonging and collaboration among students from diverse backgrounds.

NSNX builds a supportive community where students can thrive academically and socially. By organizing events, workshops, and networking opportunities, it connects students with peers, faculty, and industry professionals.

The association provides resources to help students succeed in their studies and prepare for their careers, empowering them to make the most of their time at EPFL!

Committee

The NSNX committee is composed of dedicated students who take on roles such as President, Treasurer, or Event Coordinator to ensure the smooth functioning of the association. Each member plays a vital role in supporting the Neuro-X community!

The committee is responsible for organizing events, workshops, and networking opportunities that foster collaboration and inclusivity among students. They work to create a vibrant environment where everyone feels supported and engaged.

In addition to event planning, the committee acts as a bridge between students and faculty, ensuring that feedback is heard and addressed. They also oversee commissions focused on areas like events, social media, and sponsorships. Learn more on the Commissions page.
